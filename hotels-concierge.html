What the desk asks before the first booking.

CALL 877-374-1122
How quickly can you cover a same-day guest request?

The desk reaches a live dispatcher at any hour. With cars staged across Manhattan, same-day sedan requests are usually confirmed within minutes and at the door on the guest’s schedule — including the request that comes in as the guest is putting on their coat.

Do you provide a dedicated house car?

We run house car programmes with a dedicated vehicle and chauffeur stationed at the property for agreed hours, presented to your specification, with the wider fleet behind them whenever demand exceeds the one car.

How are chauffeurs briefed on our standards?

Chauffeurs assigned to a property are trained on its arrival protocol before a first shift — greeting standards, door and luggage handling, which entrance to use, discretion expectations and the routing your team prefers at each time of day.

Do you handle VIP, celebrity and diplomatic guests?

Regularly, and quietly. Discreet arrivals, back-of-house entrances, security-trained chauffeurs and armoured vehicles are all available, coordinated with your security team and the guest’s own protection detail.

Is there a cost to the hotel?

None. There is no setup fee, no minimum volume and no commitment. A partner property receives preferred rates, a named account manager and consolidated billing — and keeps the freedom to use us as often or as rarely as the desk chooses.
