Before You Book

What bankers, EAs and publicists ask us.

CALL 877-374-1122
What makes a roadshow chauffeur different?

They come from a trained group: NDAs signed, trained to stay with the principal and the car for the full day, familiar with the service entrances and garages on a New York investor route, and briefed to coordinate with your assistant rather than the passenger.

Do we keep the same chauffeur for the whole roadshow?

Yes. The same chauffeur and the same car stay with the principal across a multi-day program, so preferences and routing are never re-explained at the start of a morning.

Can you run a five- or ten-car roadshow?

Regularly. One dispatcher holds the full manifest — lead car, principal cars and support vehicles — with matched vehicles where possible and a standby car held for the inevitable change.

Do you cover cities outside New York?

Yes. Multi-city US itineraries are covered through vetted affiliate operators, booked and billed through the same Friendly Ride dispatch desk — you keep one point of contact and one invoice for the whole tour.

How is a roadshow booked and billed?

Hourly as-directed when the cars need to hold between meetings, or as scheduled point-to-point legs when the itinerary is fixed. Corporate accounts get one consolidated invoice coded to a deal, matter number or cost center.

Will chauffeurs sign our NDA?

Confidentiality agreements are standard on roadshow assignments, and we will execute your firm's own NDA before the program begins. Certificates of insurance are issued to your risk team on request.
