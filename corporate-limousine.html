How do we open a corporate account?

One form and a credit reference. Most accounts are live within a business day, with your authorised bookers, cost centres and frequent passengers loaded before the first reservation is made.

How is corporate travel billed?

One consolidated monthly invoice on net terms, with every trip coded to the department, cost centre, matter or project number captured at booking — and a spend report attached for reconciliation.

Can assistants book for several executives?

Yes. Named assistants and travel managers hold as many passenger profiles as they need and book by portal, email or phone — always reaching a dispatcher who already has the account open.

Do you cover cities outside New York?

New York, New Jersey and Connecticut are ours directly. Beyond the tri-state area we book and supervise vetted affiliates on your account, so the itinerary, the standards and the invoice stay with us.

What insurance and vendor documents can you provide?

Certificates of insurance naming your entity, W-9, licensing and any vendor-onboarding questionnaire your procurement team uses — returned promptly, before the account goes live if you prefer.

How new are the vehicles on the account?

Current-model, 2023 through 2026 — Mercedes-Benz S-Class, Mercedes-Maybach, Cadillac Escalade ESV and executive Sprinter vans, with marque and armoured cars available on notice.
