How We Keep It

A fleet is only as good as the morning it starts.

Inspected daily, not scheduled quarterly

Mechanical condition, tyre and brake wear, cabin presentation and amenities are checked each morning before a car is released to a job.

Current model years only

Vehicles are cycled out of the fleet well before age shows. What you reserve is the current generation of that car, not a facelift from three cycles ago.

Matched for multi-car work

Multi-car programmes are filled with matching vehicles wherever the manifest allows, so a five-car movement arrives looking like one.

Vetted affiliates, never brokers

When a day outgrows what we own, we extend only through operators we have used for years — never an unknown car sent by a broker.
