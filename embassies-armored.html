Who We Work For

Missions don’t run transport. Their security firms do — and they call us.

A consulate rarely dispatches its own vehicles. The account sits with an executive protection firm, a security consultancy or a protective detail, and ground transportation is delegated. That is where we have spent twenty years — as the transport layer inside someone else’s operation.

It means we know how to take direction. Your advance dictates the route. Your detail lead sets the timing. Our chauffeurs execute it without needing to be managed, and without becoming a variable you have to account for.

The Protocol

Seven stages between the first call and the last door closing.

Diplomatic movements fail in the seams — between the advance and the driver, between the airport and the cordon. This is the sequence we run so there are no seams to fail in.

Confidentiality

Names, routes, timings and manifests are held at the operations desk and never discussed outside it. We will execute your NDA before the brief.

Stage 01
Brief taken under NDA

The detail lead or procurement officer briefs operations directly. Confidentiality is executed first — we do not ask who the principal is until the paperwork allows us to know.

Stage 02
Vehicle configuration agreed

Armored or standard, principal car and follow vehicles, seating and capacity. Armor specification is set against the assessed requirement and discussed only with you — never published.

Stage 03
Chauffeurs named and cleared

Assignments go to chauffeurs already background-checked, certified in security and evasive driving, and protocol trained. Names and license details are submitted for your own vetting before the movement.

Stage 04
Credentialing and liaison

Coordination with US State Department Diplomatic Security, NYPD and local law enforcement — cordon access, frozen zones, credential plates and approved holding positions, arranged before the day rather than argued at the barricade.

Stage 05
Arrival — tarmac or terminal

Tarmac and FBO access at Teterboro, JFK, Newark and Westchester, subject to authority approval, so the principal moves from aircraft steps to vehicle without a terminal. Otherwise a discreet meet inside, with the car already positioned.

Stage 06
The movement, to your direction

Your advance owns the route. Chauffeurs hold formation, keep the order of precedence at every door, and place flags correctly when the movement calls for them. Standby vehicles are held for the change that always comes.

Stage 07
Close-out, then nothing

Manifests reconciled, one invoice to the retaining firm, records held to your retention terms. No case studies, no client names, no photographs. The reason you can read this page is that we do not write about the work.
