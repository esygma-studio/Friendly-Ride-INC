Before You Book

Questions planners and schedulers ask first.

CALL 877-374-1122
How far ahead should we book a wedding or gala?

As soon as the date and venue are set. Peak wedding season and gala weeks fill the marque fleet first, though we can usually accommodate a meeting or single ride on short notice.

Can you run a whole wedding-day timeline?

Yes. One coordinator confirms every pickup and hold time against your run-of-show before the day, with a standby car on site to absorb weather or a changed plan.

Do you work with our wedding or event planner?

Regularly. We take routing directly from your planner and confirm it with them, not just the couple or host.

Can one car serve back-to-back meetings across a day?

Yes — book it as-directed and the same car and chauffeur hold for the whole day, moving with you between stops.

What vehicles are available for a bridal party or larger group?

From a lead car for the couple to Sprinter vans shuttling guests between venues, with full luggage hold and three rows in the Escalade ESV.

Is gratuity and decoration handled for us?

Gratuity is included in every quote. Ribbon or floral detailing on the lead car can be arranged with notice.
