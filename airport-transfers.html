Before You Fly

The questions our dispatchers hear most.

CALL 877-374-1122
Do you track my flight?

Yes — every arrival is monitored against live flight data and the chauffeur is dispatched to the adjusted landing time. A delay or an early touchdown costs you nothing.

What does the VIP Meet and Greet include?

A dedicated greeter tracks your flight, meets you at the gate or arrivals hall with a discreet name board, handles your luggage, and escorts you through immigration and baggage claim to a chauffeur who already knows your name — on every transfer, at no extra charge.

Will someone meet me inside the terminal?

Yes — at baggage claim or the customs exit, with a discreet name board and hands for the luggage. Curbside pickup is there when you would rather move quickly.

How early should the car collect me for a departure?

We set the pickup backwards from your gate time using the day’s real traffic and TSA conditions — typically three hours before an international departure and two before a domestic one.

Can you handle a delegation or several flights at once?

Regularly. Multi-car arrivals, staggered flights and consulate protocol are coordinated by one dispatcher who holds the whole manifest — see Partners & Clients.
